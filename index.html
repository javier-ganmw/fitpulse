Setting up your Fitbit API

Register a free developer app — takes about 2 minutes, no coding required.

1
Sign into the Fitbit developer portal
Go to dev.fitbit.com/apps/new and sign in with your regular Fitbit account.
2
Register a new application
Fill in the form with these values:

App Name: FitPulse
App Type: Personal
Callback URL: loading…
Access Type: Read Only
3
Copy your Client ID
After registering, copy the OAuth 2.0 Client ID — it looks like 23ABCD. That's all you need. You do not need the Client Secret.
4
Paste it into FitPulse
Close this dialog, paste your Client ID into the box, and click Connect.

Privacy: FitPulse never stores your data. Your access token lives only in your browser's sessionStorage and is cleared when you disconnect or close the tab.
